# G04 independent reference applicability — design hold

2026-10-10. No experiment is frozen or authorized by this design document.
NEXT_REQUIRED_GATE remains OL-G04_REFERENCE_TARGET_APPLICABILITY.
External governance deployment in scientific_governance/README.md blocks new
qualifying execution and expensive dataset generation.

Question: does the accepted full-SPD MFEM forecast configuration accurately
propagate representative accepted bin-free occupation densities, rather than
merely satisfying operational mass/positivity tests?

Demonstrated proposition sought: bounded reference-discretization sensitivity
for a prospectively specified finite input population and fixed forecast map.
Not demonstrated: all attractor inputs, posterior transfer, continuum proof,
learning-grid adequacy, surrogate qualification or complete assimilation.
Contribution: determines whether supervised targets are sufficiently accurate
for the eventual full-density Bayesian forecast objective.

Before a run, independently review and owner-sign: population/sampling frame,
retained difficult cases, fresh source-group allocation, law/reconstruction
contract, diffusion/domain/horizon, FE transfer and common-grid quadrature,
mesh/timestep hierarchy, primary density discrepancy functional, uncertainty
budget, acceptable target bias relative to downstream scientific error budget,
all attempt IDs, no exclusions/retries in protocol v1, required raw artifacts,
falsification tests and consequences of every failure. Thresholds remain
TO_BE_PREDECLARED_BEFORE_RUN; no retrospective success criterion is supplied.

Competing hypotheses: insufficient h resolution; timestep bias; initialization
projection; reconstruction variation; positivity interventions; hanging-face
flux defects; boundary truncation; export/coarsening error. Hold all but the
tested factor fixed. Separately measure initial representation and dynamics.
Preserve unconstrained/raw and accepted density data, complete correction and
mass histories, covariance/marginal/lobe/boundary diagnostics, optimizer outcomes,
residuals, timings, resources, command, seeds and complete source/environment.

Independent verifier must recompute mass, negative mass, full density distances,
moments, covariance, marginals, lobe/boundary probabilities from raw fields with
separate integration/metric code. Establish appropriate exact or manufactured
benchmarks and hanging-face conservation checks; compare controlled h/dt
refinements on the same physical law. Test mutations of samples, denominators,
hashes and criteria. Numerical quadrature positivity samples cannot replace
whole-cell certification; continuum claims require their own justification.

Acceptance tests and verifier must be approved outside implementation control.
Executor seals the full archive; independent operator verifies; owner approves
the exact scoped claim. Any unresolved failure retains G04 OPEN, without tuning
inputs to manufacture reference success. Passing G04 unlocks only the next
controlled gate, not a large campaign, neural model or posterior transfer.

Method attribution: controlled refinement and independent physical invariants
continue the existing FEM MATH_PROTOCOL; governance mechanism and primary
sources are recorded in scientific_governance/README.md. No published theorem
is imported as a guarantee for these new empirical law targets.

## What the laws mean

Six occupation inputs: original accepted FE densities conservatively represented
on 60×72×72; time-occupation histograms from deterministic trajectories, not
stationary-law proofs or observation-conditioned uncertainty.
Twelve block-resampled reconstructions: two per source, circular 50-sample blocks.
These measure one chosen dependent-sampling sensitivity, not independent new laws
or valid confidence intervals.
Twelve ensemble priors: 16,384 initial particles drawn from each original voxel
density, evolved to T=.05 by Euler--Maruyama under the fixed full-SPD tensor.
Two independent ensemble realizations per source, with identical reconstruction.
Whole-space SDE, no reflection; no observed path exits. This does not prove
boundary equivalence or continuum/time-discretization accuracy.
Seventy-two conditioned examples: each ensemble prior conditioned on one
independently simulated truth-derived observation, x/xz/full maps and observation
variance4 or16. The same truth and standardized observation noise are retained
across ensemble replicates and noise scales. Conditioning integrates the Gaussian
likelihood exactly against the piecewise-constant voxel prior. Full non-Gaussian
state density is retained; no Gaussian/GMM state fitting and no DA cycle.

## Interpretation and competing explanations

NUMERICAL: the current six occupation laws do not closely reproduce the specific
conditioned densities constructed here; observation conditioning materially
changes concentration and lobe mass under plausible project observation models.
The population needs these structures if eventual forecast inputs include them.
This is not a proof that every occupation generator is unsuitable or that these
examples characterize every eventual posterior.

HEURISTIC: block variation overlaps the between-law L1 range, so apparent six-law
diversity may contain substantial finite-window/reconstruction variability.
Law heterogeneity and sampling sensitivity are not separately identified with
two replicates and one block length. G02/G03 must resolve this, rather than
declaring the six examples identical or independent by assumption.

The very small occupation reconstruction-versus-initial-FE difference supports
faithfulness of this particular initial representation recipe. It does not
certify forecast target accuracy, subvoxel resolution or all posterior exports.
The ensemble-versus-occupation differences mix dynamics and regularization;
they cannot be interpreted as pure physical FPE change. G01/G02/G04 remain required.

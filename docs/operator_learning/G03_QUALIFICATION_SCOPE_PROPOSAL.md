# G03 qualification scope — owner-review proposal, not an executable gate

2026-10-08, OL-D029. DRAFT_REQUIRES_OWNER_REVIEW; G03 remains OPEN.
No retrospective pass or new run. All previous characterization criteria/results
remain unchanged. This proposal addresses the question in gates.json:
"Do examples represent genuinely distinct laws?"

## Recommended division of responsibilities

G03 should qualify scoped evidence of genuine finite-window law diversity and
a transparent candidate-population design. It should not require an optimal
training population before reference applicability and forecast performance can
be measured. Preserve training adequacy as an OPEN assumption to be investigated
at baseline/generalization gates, with G03 coverage diagnostics and limitations
carried forward. This is proposed interpretation requiring owner approval,
not permission to weaken or silently supersede existing gate expectations.

Owner-approved population remains regularized finite-window occupation laws
of recorded simulated Lorenz trajectories. Initial-state generator scope,
spinup/window duration and fixed smoothing must be declared. Universal initial-
condition forecast ability remains a research ambition, not a finite gate claim.
Actual posterior transfer remains separately locked. No Gaussian/GMM substitution.

## Draft acceptance responsibilities

1. Freeze a fresh, bounded source-group/window sample plan BEFORE execution.
   Keep all attempted laws, including duplicates and difficult cases. Define
   law/group/replicate identities and dependence; related windows cannot cross
   source-group evaluation splits. No new untouched-evaluation claim for inspected
   G03 development laws. Counts are engineering budgets, not a coverage theorem.
2. Use unchanged reconstruction controls; disclose window-local versus global
   fidelity scope. Retain every unresolved reference and sufficient-bound failure.
   The inherited1% reconstruction budget is neither a diversity quota nor a
   threshold for learning error. Population-uniform G02 qualification is forbidden.
3. Report all within-law versus within-source/cross-source distances and finite-
   control separation margins, lobe/moment/marginal/concentration/tail structure,
   sample counts, duplicates and ambiguity. Positive margins support particular
   distinctions; unresolved margins do not prove laws equal. No requirement that
   all legitimate laws differ from every other law.
4. Present a descriptive coverage/gap audit and a non-outcome-selected population
   design, with fixed queried source groups across candidate pool sizes when
   assessing contraction. No optimum, surrogate-error or universal coverage claim
   follows from nearest-input TV. Preserve difficult outliers without filtering.
5. Require explicit owner/reviewer acceptance of the scoped evidence, its
   limitations and continuation plan. A scientific diversity acceptance rule
   MUST be approved and frozen before a fresh qualification run. Do not choose a
   pair-separation quota from the existing favorable630/630 result.

Numerical diversity/population-coverage acceptance thresholds remain
TO_BE_PREDECLARED_BEFORE_RUN. An owner-approved qualitative evidence review can
instead be proposed explicitly, with its responsibilities and claim scope frozen
prospectively. Existing studies remain characterization, never re-labelled as
predeclared qualification. This file is not dispatchable and no fresh run is
authorized merely by writing it.

## Consequences and non-claims

A later scoped G03 pass would unlock G04 reference-applicability design only.
Recheck changed bin-free law-to-FE transfer and representative target accuracy
before dataset generation. No expensive campaign, trained-model qualification,
posterior transfer, DA or production follows automatically.
Training-population adequacy requires eventual untouched evaluation, strong
linear/POD/CNN/FNO comparisons, full-density/statistical diagnostics and a
cost/error objective. Finite characterization cannot establish prediction for
every Lorenz initial state; neither can a neural architecture guarantee it.

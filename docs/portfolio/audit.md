# Keeping track of the research

The research records bring together the methods, sources and outcomes so that another researcher can follow why a choice was made and what happened when it was tested. The method register links implementations to their research basis, the outcome ledger keeps the decisions, and the gap register records missing references or conditions that have not been checked. Historical sources, later additions and prospective methods are distinguished. The searchable graph helps retrieve those connections, but a connection to a paper does not establish that its assumptions apply to this solver. Failed and unresolved results stay in the record.

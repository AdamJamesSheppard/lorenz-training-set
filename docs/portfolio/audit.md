# Keeping track of the research

I have tried to bring the methods, sources and outcomes together so that the reasoning behind the experiments is easier to follow. The method register links implementations to their research basis, the outcome ledger keeps the decisions, and the gap register records missing references or conditions that have not been checked. Historical sources, later additions and prospective methods are distinguished. The searchable graph helps retrieve those connections, but a connection to a paper does not establish that its assumptions apply to this solver. Failed and unresolved results stay in the record.

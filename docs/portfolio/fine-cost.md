# What finer grading improves

Finer grading reduces mean relative positivity correction to about 0.0113%, compared with roughly 0.0831% for the uniform calculation. Its runtime is about 76.4 minutes, however, and it fails the recorded efficiency criteria. This shows why the method cannot be selected from a single favourable number. Lower correction helps describe the amount of numerical intervention, but it does not by itself measure density accuracy or justify the additional cost.

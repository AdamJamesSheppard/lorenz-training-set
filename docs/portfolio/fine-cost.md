# What finer grading improves

Finer grading reduces mean relative positivity correction to about 0.0113%, compared with roughly 0.0831% for the uniform calculation. Its runtime is about 76.4 minutes, however, and it fails the recorded efficiency criteria. For this comparison, the lower correction needs to be considered alongside the additional runtime. Lower correction helps describe the amount of numerical intervention, but it does not by itself measure density accuracy or justify the additional cost.

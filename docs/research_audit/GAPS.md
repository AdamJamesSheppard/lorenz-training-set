   are recorded by docs/REFERENCES.md. Their exact bibliographic editions,
   availability and theorem use have not been verified in this audit.

   certified as complete. CHAT-* stores the identifiable adaptive/subcell/anisotropic

3. Dedicated historical citations for CN/BE, RK4, Monte Carlo bootstrap/ESS,

   verification. A reachable citation is insufficient to import any theorem.
   Audit dimension, flux, diffusion, mesh, boundary, time integration and limiter
   hypotheses individually before asserting applicability.

   polynomial constrained QP has different mathematics. Carrillo–Liu–Yu use
   their nonlinear/nonlocal gradient-flow/DDG structure; hyperbolic invariant
   domain theory provides neighbouring architecture, not a Lorenz-FPE proof.
7. Crisan–Míguez v8 includes the Theorem4.2 erratum. Particle-filter density
   results do not automatically qualify deterministic, temporally dependent,
   fixed-bandwidth occupation mixtures. G02 budget remains unresolved.
8. Recent Itkin preprints are real primary records checked here, retained as
   preprints. Abstract descriptions include conditional mixed-block positivity;

9. Source-ledger run classifications are historical recorded metadata; source
   arrays and some referenced PDFs remain ignored/local. GitHub readers cannot
   reproduce every run from the compact ledger alone. No raw-data re-adjudication
   or independent reviewer is claimed.
10. A positive mass-one surrogate can still fail moments, lobes, tails and boundary
    statistics. Papers, ordinary CI and operational completion do not close those
    scientific failures or unlock later gates.

## Responsibility before a new pass/fail decision

Record the exact research source/version and mathematical use or state explicitly
that the method is a project derivation/engineering design. Identify unverified
hypotheses and competing explanations. Link frozen criteria/config/commit and
immutable evidence, preserve all failures, name reviewer/authority and scope.
Complete missing full-text reviews when their results are needed for a claim;
do not conceal a gap by attaching a plausible bibliography after the outcome.

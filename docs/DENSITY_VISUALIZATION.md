# Archived density visualization

Artifacts: `runs/density-visualization/20261002-saved-density/` (intentionally
ignored rendered evidence), generated by `scripts/plot_saved_density.py`.

The latest MFEM source is `runs/mfem-support-depth/20261001T224713Z/mfem/`.
Its initial and final conservative common-grid voxel averages have shape
180 x 216 x 216. `latest_initial.png`, `latest_final.png`, and the two-frame
`latest_endpoints.gif` display these fields. Intermediate MFEM fields were
not archived and are not reconstructed or interpolated.

The eleven-frame `reference_evolution.gif` uses actual saved cell averages
from `runs/mature-time-aggregated-grid-pipeline/20260915T134916Z/indicator_snapshots/`.
This is the earlier DOLFINx fine-graded reference, not the latest MFEM trajectory.
Nonuniform cell edges come from `experiments/mature-graded-fine-axes.json`;
their geometric volumes were checked against each snapshot's saved volumes.
The reference snapshots span t=0 to 0.05 at intervals of 0.005.

All panels use the full computational box [-30,30] x [-40,40] x [-10,70].
Marginals integrate all saved 3D cell averages with physical cell widths.
Color scales and view limits are fixed. The 3D clouds are reproducible
visualization samples from cell-average densities (seed 1963), rather than
solver particles or individual trajectories. Within-cell Q2 structure is
not resolved in these cell-average plots. No temporal interpolation is used.

The output manifest records input SHA-256 hashes, field dimensions, times,
masses, and measured negative masses. Across thirteen fields, mass lies in
[0.999999999995463, 1.0000000000000742]; measured negative mass is zero.
Pillow verification confirms 11 and 2 GIF frames, respectively, at 1500 x
1125 pixels. The initial PNG was visually inspected.

The initial law is the prescribed mature bimodal Gaussian-mixture posterior,
with mirrored Lorenz spin-up clusters and a z-observation analysis. Its two
lobes are visible. These pictures demonstrate that full three-dimensional
computed density fields exist; visual resemblance alone does not verify
the Lorenz operator or certify mature-density convergence. A 0.05 forecast
of this posterior should not be interpreted as a long-time attractor plot.

No PDE run was launched, and no solver or immutable source evidence changed.
